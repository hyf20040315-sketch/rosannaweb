Hello everyone,

I'm Rosanna, based in London. My background began in business, but I soon found myself drawn not only to coursework but to what lay behind the numbers—patterns in policy, the pulse of markets, and the question that followed me from Kings through Covent Garden to a café window facing The City: does that world of suits and flashing lights really belong to someone like me?

Currently, I define myself as a recorder and an explorer. From KCL lectures to the edges of Web3 and AI, my focus is on tracing meaningful connections between people, policies, and markets. My goal is to use analytical rigor and curiosity to create clarity from complexity—and to leave room for the kind of thinking that needs no noise, only attention: think of nothing, think of wind.

I habitually spot others' highlights and turn them into self-judgment: why haven't I mastered that skill? Why didn't I apply to that institution? I'm gentle to the outside world, yet harsh to myself like a strict examiner. / 承认吧，我正陷入一种「贪婪」的焦虑中。这个时代慷慨地展示了无数条通往成功的路径，让我产生了一种「我无所不能」的错觉。我习惯于捕捉他人的闪光点，并将其转化成对自己的审判：为什么我没有掌握那个技能？为什么我没有投递那家机构？我对外界极尽温柔，对自己却刻薄得像个严官。

My anxiety is a contradictory engine. It pushes me to network intensely, find internships, try Python and waterfall modeling, and polish every task toward perfection. To some extent, this push helped me carry KCL's high-intensity academics and turn hard-core projects at Huatai and CITIC into concrete results. / 我的焦虑是个矛盾的引擎。它鞭策我去疯狂 Networking，去找实习，去尝试 Python 和 Waterfall 模型，去试图把每一份课题都做得尽善尽美。某种程度上，正是这种推力，让我一步步扛下了 KCL 的高强度学业，也把华泰和中信那些硬核项目做成了真正能落地的成果。

I know this anxiety isn't healthy, yet I rely on the urgency it creates; without this push, I fear stagnation. The deepest fatigue often comes from self-doubt labeled "not having done enough." Even when exhausted, a voice still says: you should do more, you could have done 100%. / 我深知这种焦虑不健康，却又依赖它带来的紧迫感；如果没有这份推力，我会害怕自己停滞不前。最深的疲惫，往往来自那层名为「未尽全力」的自我怀疑。即便已经筋疲力尽，潜意识里还是会有个声音在说：你应该做得更多，你明明可以做到百分之百。

Right now, I'm learning to coexist with this emotion. I'm learning to accept that opportunities are infinite, but human energy is finite. I'm also learning not to treat anxiety as my only fuel, and to rebuild confidence from completed, tangible results. / 现在的我，正在试着与这种情绪共处。学会接受「机会是无限的，但人的精力是有极限的」。我也在学习，不再把焦虑当成唯一动力，而是从那些已经完成、实打实的结果里，找回继续向前的底气。
